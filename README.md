## The line

> I held 1% for twenty minutes so you could text your ex back. You're welcome. Tell the charger in the other room I loved it. Goodbye.

The phone is dying and it knows it — but instead of a boring battery warning, it spends its last percent being passive-aggressive about the one thing it was kept alive for: its owner texting an ex. The twist is the farewell: not to the owner, but to the charger sitting in the other room, the one thing that could have saved it. A loyal little device, betrayed by distance, going out with manners.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It validates the line (one line, 1–200 characters, valid UTF-8, no leading or trailing whitespace) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
